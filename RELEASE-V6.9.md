# WISH WORLD V6.9 — Social Life

- Nearby text chat for signed-in public players within 12 world units, using server-side presence and audience filtering, blocking and rate limits. 100-character limit, 45-second visible history, 24-hour cleanup on send. No voice chat/WebSocket.
- Speech bubbles, quick chat, mute toggle, message reporting and block controls. Reports reuse existing staff queue with negative message IDs to distinguish nearby chat.
- All public version labels updated to V6.9. Browser and full backend testing still pending.
