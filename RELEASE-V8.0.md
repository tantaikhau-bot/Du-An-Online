# WISH WORLD V8.0 — Real Life Simulator

Combined V7.9 graphics and V8.0 life gameplay.
- Procedural NPC facial details, clothing buttons, pet whiskers and deer markings, city planters/roof details and ceremony decorations. Keeps existing mobile graphics budgets and distance culling. Not a complete model/animation overhaul or new asset pack.
- Five daily local job contracts, resource consumption, virtual Sao and profession XP. Delivery near market, purchase and ride a local procedural bicycle with wheel animation; not synchronized as a vehicle to other players. Existing horse and houses retained.
- Server-backed free keepsake gifts to accepted friends, max five per rolling 24h. Symbolic vows only between accepted companions, both must confirm while nearby in public world. Either may cancel. Gifts visible 30 days, cleaned on gift send, blocked relationships filtered. No legal marriage, real-money rewards or shared property.
- 15 scripts parsed; seven synthetic local job/bicycle checks passed. Added Python tests (not executed). No browser/GPU/mobile or two-account integration test. SQLite storage remains ephemeral on free Render; export progress before deployment.
