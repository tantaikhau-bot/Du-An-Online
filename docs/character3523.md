# V35.2.3 — Character Shape and Sword Motion

Twenty distinct mesh deformation profiles reuse the six available licensed VRM bases; these are not 20 independently sculpted models. Preserve skinning and base height, reshape torso, hips, legs, depth, head and jaw. Ground combat stance uses actual normalized leg axes and guards against inward roll. Melee blades use a visible scabbard and transition between carry and palm grip, with bounded arm support IK. Visual quality and real gameplay require browser testing.
