# WISH WORLD V9.0 — Horizons (beta)

- New responsive garage for bicycle, motorbike and touring car. Car costs 360 local virtual Sao; purchase rollback on save failure and ledger entry. Procedural car body, wheels, lights and glass, camera pulls back; car appearance synchronized through validated presence enum. Driver avatar hidden inside enclosed car; no passengers. Uses existing player collision and pathfinding rather than dedicated vehicle physics, and local ownership is not server-authoritative.
- More varied village facade/roof colors, narrower and softer paving seams, lower road normal strength, closer adventure camera, responsive action grids and market rows. Existing animated GLB and optional photo texture loaders now accessible from settings; no new external asset pack, professional rig or model replacement. Customized avatars preserved.
- 15 inline JS scripts parsed, nine synthetic car ownership/purchase checks passed. Python car tests added, not executed. No actual 3D browser or mobile benchmark. V9.0 is a beta, not a commercial release. Shared 3D property, live ceremony animation, passenger vehicles/boats, expanded map, WebSocket and durable cloud database remain pending. Export progress before deploying to free Render.

- Ceremony guest list and RSVP: accepted pair plan with date required, invitation to accepted friends only, up to 12 guests, guest may accept/decline, host may revoke. Changes or cancellation of pair plan invalidate prior invitations. No live ceremony animation or shared room. Added backend test cases, not executed.
